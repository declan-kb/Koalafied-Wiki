# Deutsch DTM Connectors

**Overall requirement:** Show understanding and competency in making Deutsch DTM connectors.

**Explain and demonstrate the wire preparation required for Deutsch DTM connectors**

The closed Deutsch pins have very short barrels, so only ~3-5mm of wire needs to be stripped. Of all connectors, this is one where stripping slightly too much is the most okay.

**Identify the components required to construct the male and female plugs of a pair of Deutsch DTM connectors**

Each half of the connector requires five parts: two pins, a rubber seal, a shroud, and an insert. The male pins go with the female shroud, and the grey insert. The female pins go with the male shroud and orange insert.

**Demonstrate the process of crimping the Deutsch DTM pins for each half of a connector**

Once the wires have been stripped, check which side of the connection should be male/female. In general, male connectors face away from the roboRIO, towards the PDH along the CAN bus. Crimp the pins onto the wires using the Deutsch crimp tool, ensuring the pin is pushed fully into the tool.

**Demonstrate the process of constructing the male and female plugs of a Deutsch DTM connector**

Place the rubber seal on each pair of wires, ~1cm past the pins. Insert the pins into the correct shrouds, ensuring that the yellow/green match across the connection, AND to other Deutsch plugs on the same robot. Align the pins correctly within the shroud, and then add the inserts. The male insert is obvious when it works/doesn’t, but the pins can be difficult to align. The female connector is easier to align the pins, and the insert is fully seated when the green seal can be seen looking into the plug. Once the inserts are seated, push the rubber seals along the wire into the shroud. Students should be able to complete the process without prompting from a mentor, but may confirm certain steps. The mentor will provide instruction for which side is male/female and yellow/green in place of referring to an existing robot.
