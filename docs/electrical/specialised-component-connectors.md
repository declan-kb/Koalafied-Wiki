# Specialised Component Connectors

**Overall requirement:** Demonstrate competency with the range of specialised connectors associated with various core components.

## Demonstrate ability to prepare and assemble roboRIO power connector

The roboRIO power connector is a custom power plug used for the roboRIO. There are two types: the classic screw-clamp terminal, and the WAGO/piano key variant. As we use 18AWG wire to power the roboRIO, we use bare wire for both cases (approx. 15mm). The ports on the plug are labelled V (volts, positive/red) and C (control, negative/black). For the screw-clamp terminal, loosen the screws with a small flat-head screwdriver, insert the wire as far as possible, and re-tighten the screw. For the WAGO/piano key version, push the button and push the wire into the connector. Once the connector is assembled, plug it into the roboRIO and screw the plug to the RIO. Students should be able to assemble either type of roboRIO connector unprompted, and have it pass a tug test from a mentor.

## Demonstrate ability to prepare and assemble RSL power connector

The RSL power plug (RSL end, not roboRIO end), is a three-terminal screw-clamp plug, with two live ports La and Lb (positive/red) and N (neutral, negative/black). We use 18-20AWG for RSL power, so bare wire is fine, ferrules could also be used for 20AWG. To assemble the connector, perform the same steps as with the screw-terminal roboRIO power connector, but with an additional red wire between La and Lb to connect them together. Once the connector is assembled, plug it into the RSL. Students should be able to assemble the RSL plug unprompted and have it pass a tug test from a mentor.

## Demonstrate ability to connect wires to OrangePi power supply terminals

The OrangePi power supply uses a different screw terminal, one that requires bare wire (15-20mm). To connect a wire to the power supply, undo the screw, wrap the wire around the screw (under the washer), and redo the screw. Students should be able to connect a wire pair to the power supply unprompted and have it pass a tug test from a mentor.
