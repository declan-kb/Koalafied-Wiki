# Spade Connectors

**Overall requirement:** Show competency in preparing and attaching spade connectors for limit switches.

**Explain the considerations for the wires that use spade connectors**

Spade connectors are used to connect to limit switches. These are often small wires at the end of a mechanism, meaning they are one of the most likely wires to be snagged/broken if something goes wrong. Therefore, these connections have some additional steps to help make them stronger.

**Explain and demonstrate the wire preparation required for spade connectors**

To prepare a wire for a spade connector, the wire needs to be stripped back so the appropriate amount of wire is exposed. The connector has features that indicate this length, similar to those seen in the Anderson Powerpole crimping guide.

**Demonstrate the process of crimping and soldering spade connectors to prepared wires**

To crimp the connector, place the wire in the crimp. To align the connector in the crimping tool, the ‘spade’ should be inside the block past the crimping jaws. Squeeze the crimp closed. Finalising the crimp can be done using pliers if necessary. After the crimp is on the wire, a small amount of solder is added to help increase the strength of the joint. Students should be able to complete the process without mentor assistance.

**Demonstrate understanding of how to implement strain relief once limit switches are connected**

Because the spade connector does not hold very strongly to the limit switch (locking spade connectors help but aren’t always sufficient/available), strain relief is used to reduce the amount of force that is applied on the connection. Simply, strain relief is implemented by clamping the wire at some point near the limit switch, while allowing some slack in the wire between that point and the limit switch, typically with a cable tie. Since the wire cannot slide through the clamping point, any force on the rest of the wire doesn’t pull on the connection. Students should be able to demonstrate strain relief on a mock limit switch.
