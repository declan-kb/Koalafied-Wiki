# Design Rules

These are rules of design that we try to live by. Remember that no rule applies 100% of the time, but if you are not following the rule you need to think about it and have a good reason.

## Top Level Robot Design

**Intakes are Hard** - Intakes have to deal with a different game piece each year so it is harder to reuse previous designs. Each year requires tuning. Also intakes need to be the most reliable part of the robot (except maybe the drivebase).

**Small Robots Are Good** - Given the choice make the robot small. It will play the game better by being lighter, accelerating faster and fitting through gaps in defence play. Also we have to go a long way to compete and a small robot is easier to transport, especially on a plane.

## Mechanical Details

**No Tee Gussets** - Tee gussets allow getting things right into the corner between two pieces of extrusion, so they are sometimes required. However, normally the gusset should ‘fill in the triangle’, because that is much, much stronger.

**Hard Stops** - Mechanisms should have hard stops to prevent unlimited movement. This helps to protect the mechanism and especially delicate things like wiring and limit switches.

## Electrical Details

**Limit Switches** - Thick wires. Latched spade connectors. Wire strain relief. See [Spade Connectors](../electrical/spade-connectors).
